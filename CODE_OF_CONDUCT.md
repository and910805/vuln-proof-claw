# Code of Conduct

[繁體中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-TW.md) | **English**

## Our standard

Project participation must be professional, respectful, and focused on safe, authorized security engineering.

Expected behavior includes constructive feedback, technical honesty, respect for privacy and consent, clear disclosure of conflicts, and welcoming contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, credential sharing, publication of private vulnerability details without coordination, encouragement of unauthorized access, and deliberate misrepresentation of evidence or authorship.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or permanently ban contributors when necessary to protect the community and project.

Enforcement decisions should be documented privately, proportionate, and free from conflicts of interest. A maintainer involved in a dispute should recuse themselves when another maintainer is available.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces, project communication channels, events, and public conduct performed while representing the project.
